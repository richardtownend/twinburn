Twin-engine roguelike racing
TWIN
BURN

Three sectors of canyon, scrapyard, ice, salt, city, storm and lava. Thread the gaps, ram the field, keep your engines out of the red. Lose your hull or your reputation and the run is over.

Turn your phone sideways for the best view.

Phone
Left thumb drives the left engine, right thumb the right. Slide up for thrust. Ease one off to turn that way.
Red band
Overdrive. Extra push, but the engine heats up and flames out at the top.
Bottom band
Airbrake on that side. Drop one lever into it for the hardest turns.
Zones
Each zone has its own trick: canyon jumps, crushers in the yards, snow flurries on the rift, fallen trunks in the forest, a lights-out tunnel, volcanic bombs, wall-less salt flats, split-level streets in the city, ship graveyards to fork through, cranes dropping containers on the storm causeway, open-edged sky platforms where going over costs you dearly, and a swamp serpent that slams down across the causeway.
Gaps
Blue-lit gaps are the safe way through: a small bonus and some engine cooling. Gold gaps are tight, pay big and dump all the heat. Anything you hit at speed smashes, costing hull and speed.
Keys
←/→ cut that engine to turn, Space for overdrive, ↓ for airbrakes. C changes camera, P pauses.
Pad
Left and right sticks (or triggers) are the two levers.
